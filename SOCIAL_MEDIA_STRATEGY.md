## Crisis Management Protocol

**Response Escalation**:
1. **Level 1**: Community manager responds (routine comments/DMs)
2. **Level 2**: Social media lead reviews (negative feedback, complaints)
3. **Level 3**: Marketing manager handles (brand reputation issues)
4. **Level 4**: CEO/CTO involved (major crises, media attention)

**Response Template for Negative Feedback**:
```
"Thank you for bringing this to our attention. We take your feedback seriously.
We'd like to help resolve this. Please DM us with more details, and our team 
will follow up within 24 hours. We appreciate your patience and support!"
```
